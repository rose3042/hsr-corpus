GB/T 36505—2018

Terms for railway permanent way

1 Scope

This standard specifies the basic terms and definitions for railway permanent way.

This standard is applicable to the engineering survey. design. construction and maintenance of rail-
way permanent way, as well as related research and training.

2 General terms

2.1
permanent way

railway line for train to travel at a specified speed, composed of track, land-base, bridge, culvert,
tunnel and other structure

2.2
standard gauge railway

railway with a gauge of 1 435 mm in straight section

2.3
broad gauge railway

railway with a gauge greater than the standard one in straight section

2.4
narrow gauge railway

railway with a gauge less than the standard one in straight section

2.5
passenger-freight line

railway for both passengers and freight transport

GB/T 36505—2018

2.6
dedicated freight line

railway mainly for freight transport

2.7
main line

line connecting and running through a station or extending into a station by a switch

2.8
station track

tracks in the station other than the main line. including arrival-departure track, formation track,
goods track, shunting neck and other lines designated for use within the station

2.9
connecting line

line connecting two stations (yards). station (yard) and main line or two main lines

2. 10
single line
single track

railway with only one main line, usually for bidirectional train operation
2.11

double line
double tracks

railway with two main lines, usually for unidirectional train operation

2.12
centre line of track

bisector of track gauge(Regardless of gauge widening value)

2.13
distance between centres of tracks

distance between the centre lines of two neighboring tracks

2.14
structure clearance

limit cross-sectional contour dimension line perpendicular to the center line of the track, into which
no other equipment or buildings. except for rolling stock and equipment directly interacting with the
rolling stock, are allowed to intrude

2.15
permanent way signs

signs on railway permanent way that indicate the permanent way mileage. the location of the track
and bridge equipment or main technical parameters and the boundaries of railway administrations at
all levels

2.16
grade crossing
level crossing

crossing of a railway with a railway or road on the same plane

2.17
grade separation

crossing of a railway with a railway or road on the different plane

3 Railway alignment

3.1
railway alignment

section

3.3

3.6
circular curve

track

5 Continuous welded rail (CWR)

tween long rail strings and other rail structures
