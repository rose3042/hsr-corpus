COMMISSION REGULATION (EU) 2025/54 
of 7 January 2025
reopening the fishery for redfish in Norwegian waters of 1 and 2 by vessels flying the flag of France, 
by repealing Regulation (EU) 2024/2815 
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,
Having regard to Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 of 20 November 2009 establishing a Union control system for 
ensuring compliance with the rules of the common fisheries policy, amending Regulations (EC) No 847/96, (EC) 
No 2371/2002, (EC) No 811/2004, (EC) No 768/2005, (EC) No 2115/2005, (EC) No 2166/2005, (EC) No 388/2006, (EC) 
No 509/2007, (EC) No 676/2007, (EC) No 1098/2007, (EC) No 1300/2008, (EC) No 1342/2008 and repealing 
Regulations (EEC) No 2847/93, (EC) No 1627/94 and (EC) No 1966/2006 (1), and in particular Article 36(2) thereof,
Whereas:
(1) 
Council Regulation (EU) 2024/257 (2) lays down quotas for 2024.
(2) 
On 10 October 2024, France notified the Commission, pursuant to Article 35(3) of Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009, 
of its decision to prohibit fishing for redfish in Norwegian waters of 1 and 2 as from 26 July 2024.
(3) 
Commission Regulation (EU) 2024/2815 (3) prohibited fishing for redfish in Norwegian waters of 1 and 2 for vessels 
flying the flag of France.
(4) 
According to the information received by the Commission on 8 November 2024 from the French authorities, a 
quantity is again available to France for the quota for that stock following exchanges of fishing opportunities 
concluded under Article 16(8) of Regulation (EU) No 1380/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council (4). 
Consequently, the fishery for redfish in Norwegian waters of 1 and 2 by vessels flying the flag of or registered in 
France should be reopened until further notice.
(5) 
That reopening of fishery should take effect on 8 November 2024, in order to allow the quantity of redfish in 
question to be fished before the end of the current year.
(6) 
Regulation (EU) 2024/2815 should therefore be repealed with effect from 8 November 2024.
(7) 
Such retroactive application does not affect the principle of legal certainty and protection of legitimate expectations 
as a quota is again available for that stock,

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:
Article 1
The fishery for redfish in Norwegian waters of 1 and 2 for the 2024 fishing season by vessels flying the flag of or registered 
in France referred to in the Annex is reopened.
Regulation (EU) 2024/2815 is repealed.
Article 2
This Regulation shall enter into force on the day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
It shall apply from 8 November 2024.
This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
Done at Brussels, 7 January 2025.
For the Commission,
On behalf of the President,
Costas KADIS
