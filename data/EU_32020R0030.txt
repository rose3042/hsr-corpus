COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2020/30
of 14 January 2020
amending Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 as regards detailed rules for the direct
electronic exchange of information enacted under the rules of the Common Fisheries Policy
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,
ensuring compliance with the rules of the common fisheries policy, amending Regulations (EC) No 847/96, (EC)
No 509/2007, (EC) No 676/2007, (EC) No 1098/2007, (EC) No 1300/2008, (EC) No 1342/2008 and repealing
Regulations (EEC) No 2847/93, (EC) No 1627/94 and (EC) No 1966/2006 (), and in particular Articles 71, 76 and 111
thereof,
Whereas:
(1)
Rules governing the exchange of data in electronic format between Member States, and between Member States and
the Commission or the body designated by it were established in Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 (the Control
Regulation'). Articles 71, 76 and 83 of that Regulation specifically concern the exchange of inspection and
surveillance reports.
(2)
The Commission has developed a new format for the transmission of inspection and surveillance report data that
should be used for all future electronic data exchanges referred to in Articles 71, 76 and 83 of the Control
Regulation.
(3)
Chapter Ia of Title IX of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 (2) lays down the rules governing
the exchange of data between Member States, and between Member States and the Commission or the body
designated by it. These rules need to be modified in order to take account of the new format developed by the
Commission for inspection and surveillance data as well as new technologies and international standards.
(4)
Since Council Regulation (EC) No 1006/2008 (3) has been repealed by Regulation (EU) 2017/2403 of the European
Parliament and of the Council (t), references to this Regulation in Chapter Ia of Title IX of Implementing Regulation
(EU) No 404/2011 should be removed.
(5)
Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 should therefore be amended accordingly
(6)
The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee for Fisheries and
Aquaculture,
OJ L 343, 22.12.2009, p. 1.
Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 of 8 April 2011 laying down detailed rules for the implementation of
Council Regulation (EC) No 1224/2009 establishing a Community control system for ensuring compliance with the rules of the
Common Fisheries Policy (OJ L 112, 30.4.2011, p. 1).
(3) Council Regulation (EC) No 1006/2008 of 29 September 2008 concerning authorisations for fishing activities of Community fishing
vessels outside Community waters and the access of third country vessels to Community waters, amending Regulations (EEC)
No 2847/93 and (EC) No 1627/94 and repealing Regulation (EC) No 3317/94 (OJ L 286, 29.10.2008, p. 33).
 Regulation (EU) 2017/2403 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 12 December 2017 on the sustainable management of
external fishing fleets, and repealing Council Regulation (EC) No 1006/2008 (OJ L 347, 28.12.2017, p. 81).

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:
Article 1
Amendments to Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011
Implementing Regulation (EU) No 404/2011 is amended as follows:
(a) Article 146a is replaced by the following:
'Article 146a
This Chapter lays down detailed rules for the exchange of data as referred to in Articles 111 and 116 of the Control
Regulation, the exchange of inspection and surveillance report data as referred to in Articles 71, 76 and 83 of the
Control Regulation, as well as for the notification of catch data as referred to in Article 33(2) and (4) of the Control
Regulation.'
(b) In Article 146i, paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:
Flag Member States shall use the XML Schema Definition based on the UN/CEFACT P1000-12 as format to
transmit to the Commission the aggregated catch data referred to in Article 33(2) and (4) of the Control Regulation:
(c) In Article 146i, paragraph 3 is replaced by the following:
‘3.The catch report quantities shall be based on the quantities landed. If catches are not yet landed, an estimate catch
report shall be provided, with the indication 'retained on board'. A correction with the exact weight and landing place
shall be transmitted before the 1 5th of the month following the landing'
(d) In Article 146j, paragraph 1 is replaced by the following:
‘1.Amendments to XML formats and implementation documents to be used for all electronic data exchanges
between Member States, and between Member States, the Commission or the body designated by it, including the
amendments resulting from Articles 146f, 146g, 146h and 146k, shall be decided by the Commission in concert with
Member States.'
(e) The following Article 146k is added:
'Article 146k
Exchange of inspection and surveillance related data
of the Control Regulation, between Member States, as well as between Member States and the Commission or the
body designated by it, shall be the Inspection and Surveillance Domain XML Schema Definition based on the UN/
CEFACT P1000-8.
2.As from a date established in concert with Member States in accordance with Article 146j(2), Member State
systems shall be capable of sending inspection and surveillance messages and of replying to requests for inspection
and surveillance data in accordance with the Inspection and Surveillance Domain XML Schema Definition based on
the UN/CEFACT P1000-8.
(f) In Annex XII, under 'P1000 - 7; Vessel Position domain', the following line is inserted:
'P1000 - 8; Inspection and Surveillance domain'.

Entry into force

This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
