Terminology for railway station and terminal
Subject and scope
This standard specifies terms commonly used for railway station and terminal。
This standard is applicable to the design , construction , operation of railway stations and terminals be- longing to national , local and industrial enterprises, as Well as scientific research, teaching , publication and other aspects

3.2 railway station
3.2.1
intermediate station
station that is generally located between two technical stations to handle reception and departure and passing of trains, shunting of wayside freight trains, transport of passenger and goods, etc。 and is equipped with arrival-departure tracks and passenger and freight facilities
3.2.2
district station

passenger station
