REGULATIONS 
COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2020/540 
of 21 January 2020 
correcting the Polish language version of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/208 supplementing 
Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to vehicle 
functional safety requirements for the approval of agricultural and forestry vehicles 
(Text with EEA relevance) 
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, 
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, 
Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 5 February 2013 on the 
approval and market surveillance of agricultural and forestry vehicles (1), in particular Article 17(5) and Article 49(3) 
thereof, 
Whereas: 
(1) 
The Polish language version of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/208 (2), contains an error in the second 
paragraph of point 6.19.7.1 of Annex XII as regards the situations where the daytime running lamps are to switch off 
automatically, thus affecting the scope of the obligations of the addressees of the erroneous provision. 
(2) 
The Polish language version of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/208 should therefore be corrected accordingly. The 
other language versions are not affected, 
HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: 
Article 1 
(does not concern the English language) 
Article 2 
This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the 
European Union. 
(1) OJ L 60, 2.3.2013, p. 1. 
(2) Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/208 of 8 December 2014 supplementing Regulation (EU) No 167/2013 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council with regard to vehicle functional safety requirements for the approval of agricultural and forestry 
vehicles (OJ L 42, 17.2.2015, p. 1).
