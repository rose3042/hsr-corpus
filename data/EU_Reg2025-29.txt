COMMISSION DELEGATED REGULATION (EU) 2025/29 
of 30 October 2024
repealing Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1235 supplementing Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the 
European Parliament and of the Council with rules concerning applications for registration of 
geographical indications of spirit drinks, amendments to product specifications, cancellation of the 
registration and the register 
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,
Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2019/787 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 17 April 2019 on the 
definition, description, presentation and labelling of spirit drinks, the use of the names of spirit drinks in the presentation 
and labelling of other foodstuffs, the protection of geographical indications for spirit drinks, the use of ethyl alcohol and 
distillates of agricultural origin in alcoholic beverages, and repealing Regulation (EC) No 110/2008 (1), and in particular 
Article 41 thereof,
Whereas:
(1) 
Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 of the European Parliament and of the Council (2) establishes one common Union 
framework for the registration and protection of geographical indications in three agricultural sectors (wine, spirit 
drinks and agricultural products). To this purpose, it amends or repeals, to the extent necessary, Regulations, which 
previously provided for separate frameworks in those sectors: Regulation (EU) No 1308/2013 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council (3) has been amended as regards geographical indications in the wine sector, 
Regulation (EU) 2019/787 has been amended as regards geographical indications in the spirit drinks sector and 
Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council (4), concerning geographical 
indications in the agricultural products and foodstuffs sector, has been repealed.
(2) 
Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 empowers the Commission to adopt delegated and implementing acts setting out 
uniform procedure for the registration of geographical indications for the agricultural sectors of wine, spirit drinks 
and agricultural products. As a result, delegated and implementing acts previously adopted on the basis of the 
empowerments given in the sectoral regulations have to be amended or repealed, to the extent necessary to bring 
them in conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 and its secondary legislation.

(3) 
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1235 (5) supplements Regulation (EU) 2019/787, among others, with 
rules concerning applications for registration of geographical indications of spirit drinks, amendments to the 
product specifications, cancellation of the registration and the register. In the new Union framework for 
geographical indications, established by Regulation (EU) 2024/1143, covering three agricultural sectors (wine, spirit 
drinks and agricultural products), the rules included in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1235 are exhaustively 
replaced, partly by certain provisions included in Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 and partly by the provisions included 
in Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/27 (6), that supplements Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 with rules 
concerning, among others, the procedures for Union amendments, and for approval and communication of 
standard and temporary amendments. Therefore, as regards the spirit drinks sector, Delegated Regulation 
(EU) 2021/1235 became obsolete and should be repealed as its provisions would be in conflict with the provisions 
adopted in Regulation (EU) 2024/1143 or in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/27, which also apply to the spirit 
drinks sector,
HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:
Article 1
Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/1235 is repealed.
Article 2
This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European 
Union.
This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States.
Done at Brussels, 30 October 2024.
