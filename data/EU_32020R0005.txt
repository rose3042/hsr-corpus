approving non-minor amendments to the specification for a name entered in the register of

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,
quality schemes for agricultural products and foodstuffs ('), and in particular Article 52(2) thereof,
Whereas:
(1)
Pursuant to the first subparagraph of Article 53(1) of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012, the Commission has
examined France's application for the approval of amendments to the specification for the protected designation of
origin Olives noires de la Vallée des Baux-de-Provence', registered under Commission Regulation (EC)
No 378/1999 (2).
(2)
Since the amendments in question are not minor within the meaning of Article 53(2) of Regulation (EU)
No 1151/2012, the Commission published the amendment application in the Official Journal of the European
Union (3) as required by Article 50(2)(a) of that Regulation.
(3)
As no statement of opposition under Article 51 of Regulation (EU) No 1151/2012 has been received by the
Commission, the amendments to the specification should be approved,
HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:
Article 1
The amendments to the specification published in the Oficial Journal of the European Union regarding the name 'Olives
noires de la Vallée des Baux-de-Provence' (PDO) are hereby approved.
