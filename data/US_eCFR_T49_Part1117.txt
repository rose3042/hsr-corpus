PART 1117-PETITIONS (FOR RELIEF) NOT OTHERWISE COVERED Authority: 49 U.S.C. 1321. &#xA7; 1117.1 Petitions. A party seeking relief not provided for in any other rule may file a petition for such relief. The petition should contain (a) a short, plain statement of the grounds upon which the Board's jurisdiction is based; (b) a short plain statement of the claim showing that the petitioner is entitled to relief; and (c) a demand for the relief the petitioner believes is appropriate.
