GB/T 36543—2018

Terms for railway bridge

1 Scope

This standard specifies the basic terms and definitions for railway bridge engineering structures.

This standard is applicable to railway bridge engineering structures.
2 Bridge

2.1
railway bridge

structure that allows a railway to cross a natural obstacle or a man-made facility

2.2
jacked-in bridge or culvert

bridge or culvert that crosses the existing railway earthworks and is constructed by jacking method

2.3
bridge superstructure

structure that is located above the bearing of a girder bridge or above the arch line of an arch bridge

and spans a bridge opening

2.4
steel bridge

bridge whose superstructure is mainly made of steel

2.5
reinforced concrete bridge

bridge whose superstructure is mainly made of concrete containing tensioned steel bar

GB/T 36543—2018

2.6
prestressed concrete bridge

bridge whose superstructure is mainly made of prestressed concrete

2.7
stone bridge

bridge whose superstructure is made of stone

2.8
girder bridge

bridge that uses girder as superstructure

2.9
arch bridge

bridge that uses arch ring or arch rib as superstructure

2. 10
rigid frame bridge

bridge whose superstructure is rigidly connected to piers and abutments

2.11
frame bridge

bridge whose main load-bearing member is designed as an integrated box frame

2.12
slant-legged rigid frame bridge

rigid frame bridge supported by slant columns to increase the span of the bridge

2.13
suspension bridge

bridge whose deck is hung by vertical suspenders on cables which are suspended between towers
and connected to the anchorages at each end of the girders

GB/T 36543—2018

2.14
cable-stayed bridge

bridge whose superstructure comprises towers and main girders which are connected by stay cables

2.15
movable bridge

bridge where one or two spans of the superstructure may be lifted. turned horizontally or vertically

2.16
pontoon (floating) bridge

bridge supported by civil ships, standard boats or pontoon boxes as floating piers over which the su-
perstructure is erected

2.17
deck bridge

bridge whose deck is located above the superstructure

2. 18
half-through bridge

bridge whose deck passes through the superstructure

2.19
through bridge

bridge whose deck is located below the superstructure

2.20
single-track bridge

bridge with only one rail laid

2.21
double-track bridge

bridge with two rails laid

GB/T 36543—2018

2.22
multi-track bridge

bridge with more than two rails laid

2.23
viaduct

multi-span bridge built to replace an embankment to cross a deep valley. low-lying land. man-made
facility

2.24
rail-cum-road bridge

bridge for both road and railway traffic
2.25

fly over bridge

grade separated bridge

bridge that crosses railways. highways. urban roads. etc.

2. 26
bridge with turnout

bridge with a rail turnout on it

2.27
bridge with slab track

bridge with slab track on the deck

2. 28
main bridge

bridge that crosses the main channel of a river or a deep valley or the main part of a man-made facility

2.29
approach bridge

bridge that connects the embankment and the main bridge

3 Beam and arch

3.1
beam

straight or curve members, mainly bearing the bending moment and shear force produced by various
loads

3.2
truss

planar or spatial grid structure composed of several members that mainly bear the axial force
produced by various loads

3.3
main girder

girder that supports various loads in the superstructure and transmits them to piers and abutments

3.4
simply supported beam

beam rested on two end bearings, one of which is an axially constrained hinge bearing and the other
is an axially rolling bearing

3.5
continuous beam

3.6

3.7

top

steel box girder

single-chamber or multi-chamber box girder composed of steel plates reinforced by longitudinal or
